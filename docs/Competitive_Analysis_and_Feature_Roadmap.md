## PART 1 — COMPETITIVE ANALYSIS

### Market structure note (important)
Airtasker **acquired Oneflare for $9.8M** — so two of your three "competitors" are one company now, and insiders report Oneflare has been slowed down significantly since. The field is really: HiPages (ASX-listed leader) vs Airtasker group. Both are lead/task marketplaces. **Neither does financial risk, builder-to-tradie hiring, or community.**

### HiPages — the leader
**Model:** Subscription ($109–$419/month tiers) + pay-per-lead on top. Lead credits expire in 2–3 months. Only 3 tradies can buy each lead (scarcity). 6-month lock-in contracts. ~1.5M monthly visitors, a job posted every 23 seconds.

**What they do well (steal these):**
- **TradieCore app** — quoting, scheduling, invoicing built in → makes the platform sticky beyond leads.
- **Instant push notifications** — first tradie to respond usually wins; speed mechanics drive engagement.
- **Lead cap (3 per job)** — scarcity keeps leads valuable and tradies paying.
- **Profile-as-asset** — 30+ reviews meaningfully lifts conversion; tradies invest in profiles and can't leave.

**Where tradies hate them (avoid these):**
- Paying for dud leads — up to $200/lead, tyre-kickers, leads sold to more tradies than advertised. THE #1 resentment in the market.
- Credits expiring with no rollover.
- Lock-in contracts + hard cancellation (stat dec required!) — ACCC complaints.
- Review suppression — customers report negative reviews don't appear → trust damage.
- Race-to-the-bottom pricing: 3 quotes side-by-side = cheapest wins.

### Airtasker — the open marketplace
**Model:** Zero upfront fees; 10–30% commission (typically ~15%) taken from the worker's payment on completion. Escrow-style **Airtasker Pay** — money held on platform, released on completion. Contact details blocked until booking (protects the commission).

**What they do well (steal these):**
- **Escrow payments** — customer's money is secured, worker knows funds exist. Trust machine + the platform's take is automatic.
- **$10M public liability cover** on platform-completed tasks — powerful trust signal (with fine print).
- **Zero-upfront model** — no barrier to tradie signup; you only pay when you earn. The opposite of HiPages resentment.
- Automatic record of agreements (price changes need consent in-app) — dispute protection.

**Where they're weak (avoid):**
- "Double-dipping" fees (charging both sides) — top complaint.
- Weak vetting → no-shows, unqualified workers, safety incidents.
- AI-only support, no humans in disputes — huge dissatisfaction.

### Oneflare — the verification pioneer (now absorbed)
**Model:** Pay-per-lead via credits (~$1/credit, leads from ~$26). Up to 3 quotes per job.

**What they did well (steal these):**
- **Verification badges** — "Verified" = ABN + public liability insurance confirmed. Directly validates YOUR badge concept — except yours adds the layer nobody has: financial health.
- **Guarantee badges** — "On-Time Guarantee" ($50 reimbursement), "Home Care" ($500 cover). Micro-guarantees = cheap trust.
- **Cost guides** — "what does a tiler cost?" content → massive SEO traffic → free customer acquisition. This is how you get homeowners without ad spend.
- **They literally bought a community** — acquired Renovate Forum (40,000 contributors) and WOMO (420,000 reviews) to build trust and traffic. Your community instinct is the same play, built organically.

### The verdict — your positioning in one line
All three sell **introductions**. Nobody sells **safety**. And nobody serves **builder→tradie** — they're all consumer→tradie only. Your platform = safety layer + the B2B side they ignore + community. You can even coexist with them at first: a tradie can keep buying HiPages leads AND pay you to check the builders behind them.

## PART 2 — EXPANDED FEATURE ROADMAP

### NEW: Builder → Tradie Hiring Board (your idea — and it's strong)
The gap: HiPages/Airtasker connect *homeowners* to tradies. **Nobody properly serves builders finding subbies and day labour** — and the industry is short ~300,000 workers by mid-2027. Builders currently beg in Facebook groups.

**Features:**
- **Job posts by builders:** ongoing subcontract work OR day-hire ("need 2 tile layers, tomorrow, Cranbourne, $X/day").
- **"Available Now" toggle for tradies** — a tradie with a rained-off day flips it on, sees day work near them. (Day-work urgency = daily app opens = engagement gold.)
- **Trust runs BOTH ways — this is the killer integration no competitor can copy:**
  - Tradie sees the builder's **risk status** before accepting work ("will I actually get paid?").
  - Builder sees the tradie's **verified licence + insurance + reviews** before hiring.
- **Reliability score** for day workers (turned up, on time, rehired %).
- Monetisation: free to browse; builders pay to post ($15–30/post or subscription); later, optional escrowed day-pay (Airtasker-style) = commission revenue.

⚖️ *Legal light-touch:* you're a **noticeboard, not a labour-hire agency** — parties contract directly, workers are ABN contractors engaging on their own terms. Labour-hire *licensing* (VIC has a scheme) applies to businesses that *supply* workers — platform-as-noticeboard sits outside it, but keep the model clean: never set pay, never direct work, never invoice for the labour. Add to the lawyer session checklist. Also flag sham-contracting basics in help content.

### NEW: Community Layer (your instinct, validated by Oneflare buying a forum)
- **Trade feed / forum:** "builder not paying" threads, subbie advice, war stories, wins. This content is *exactly* what fills Facebook groups today — give it a home next to the tools that solve it.
- **Collapse watch news feed:** every industry collapse reported + "the signals that showed first." Becomes the industry's must-follow feed.
- **Q&A with experts:** partner lawyer answers one payment-dispute question weekly (content + lead-gen for them, authority for you).
- **Homeowner side (Phase 2):** renovation diaries, cost guides, "questions to ask before paying a deposit."
- ⚖️ *Moderation duty:* under Australian defamation law YOU can be liable as publisher of users' posts — active moderation, house rules, fast takedown process are mandatory, not optional (already in Master Plan §4.1 spirit — extend it to UGC).

### Features to adopt from competitors
| Feature | From | Phase |
|---|---|---|
| Verification badges (ABN/licence/insurance) — then financial tier on top | Oneflare | 1–2 |
| Cost guides for SEO traffic | Oneflare | 2 |
| Instant push notifications for jobs/alerts | HiPages | 1 |
| Quoting/invoicing mini-tools (stickiness) | HiPages TradieCore | 3 |
| Lead cap per job (scarcity) | HiPages/Oneflare | 2 |
| Escrow payments + platform records | Airtasker | 3 (payments = licensing/compliance weight — later) |
| Micro-guarantees (on-time, etc.) | Oneflare | 3 |

### Their mistakes = your rules
1. **Never charge for dud leads.** Subscriptions and job-post fees, not per-lead gambling — you inherit none of the #1 market resentment.
2. **No lock-in contracts.** Cancel anytime. Advertise it loudly — it's a weapon against HiPages.
3. **Never suppress negative reviews.** Verified-transaction reviews only, both directions (tradies rate builders too — nobody lets them do that today).
4. **Humans in disputes.** Even if it's just you at first — Airtasker's AI-only support is despised.
5. **Don't double-dip.** Be transparent about who pays what.

### My additional suggestions (worth adding when their phase comes)
- **Deposit-safety check for homeowners (Phase 2):** before paying a builder deposit, run the check + get plain-English guidance on VIC domestic-building deposit limits etc. Emotional hook of the whole consumer side.
- **Exposure alerts tied to dollars:** "You've logged $42K exposure to a builder that just got a court filing" — the alert that makes the subscription unquestionable.
- **Insurance-expiry tracking:** tradies/builders get renewal reminders; verified profiles auto-flag when insurance lapses (keeps badge integrity honest).
- **WhatsApp/SMS-first alerts:** tradies don't check email.
- **Referral engine:** a subbie whose builder flags red tells every other subbie on that site. Build "invite your crew" into the alert moment itself — one tap shares the warning (public-record facts only) to their mates → viral loop at the exact peak of emotion.

## PART 3 — UPDATED PHASING (discipline check)

**Phase 1 (the 4-day sprint — UNCHANGED):** risk engine, watchlists, exposure tracker, free builder check, subscriptions. *This funds everything else.*

**Phase 1.5 (weeks 3–6):** Builder→tradie job board MVP (posts + Available Now toggle only — no payments), because it's simple to build, feeds daily engagement, and starts the builder-side network.

**Phase 2 (months 2–4):** builder accounts + badges, community feed, cost guides, homeowner directory.

**Phase 3 (months 4+):** escrow payments, SOPA claims engine (with lawyer partner), workflow tools, guarantees.

**The one-sentence strategy:** *enter as the safety layer nobody has, expand into the marketplace everybody uses.*
