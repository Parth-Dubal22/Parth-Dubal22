## PART 2 — OUR PLATFORM: COMPLETE PAGE MAP

### 🌐 Public website (SEO + conversion)
1. **Homepage** — hero: "Check any builder in 10 seconds. Free." + live trust bar (X builders monitored, $X exposure protected) + how-it-works 3 steps + trust block.
2. **Free Builder Check page** — the flagship tool. ABN/name search → instant facts report. Every result page = shareable + indexable (SEO compounding).
3. **Builder profile pages** (public) — licence status, verification tier, reviews, work photos, "claim this profile" CTA. *(Positive/neutral info only — risk detail stays private, per defamation design.)*
4. **Directory pages** — /find/{trade}/{suburb} grid (HiPages' SEO empire, rebuilt).
5. **Job board (public feed)** — live builder→tradie posts with $ and dates (Airtasker's public-feed FOMO pattern).
6. **Cost guides + Checklists** — "Tiler day rate 2026", "Checklist before paying a builder deposit", "What is SOPA". SEO engine.
7. **Collapse Watch (news/blog)** — industry collapse coverage + "the signals that showed first." Our authority engine.
8. **For Tradies page** — pitch + pricing (no lock-ins, cancel anytime — say it loud).
9. **For Builders page** — hire tradies + get verified pitch.
10. **For Homeowners page** (Phase 2) — deposit-safety angle.
11. **Exposure Calculator** (lead magnet, Airtasker's calculator pattern) — "How much of YOUR money is riding on your builders?"
12. **Trust & Safety page** — how verification works, review integrity policy, dispute/correction process (public defamation shield).
13. **Community guidelines** — day 1 (moderation duty).
14. **About / Contact / Support** — with a phone number or fast human reply; competitors' worst area is support, make it a weapon.
15. **Legal set** — T&Cs, Privacy Policy, disclaimers.

### 📱 App — Tradie side
- **Dashboard:** watchlist status tiles + total $ exposure + latest alerts.
- **Watchlist:** add builder by ABN/name; per-builder signal history.
- **Exposure tracker:** amounts owed/deposits per builder.
- **Alerts inbox:** push/SMS/email; every alert = fact + source link + "what you can consider" (info, not advice).
- **Job board:** browse builder posts; filters (trade, distance, date, day-rate); **Available Now toggle**; one-tap apply.
- **My profile:** trades, licence, insurance (with expiry reminders), work photos, reliability score, reviews.
- **Invite your crew:** share a builder's public-record alert with mates (viral loop).
- **Community feed:** threads, collapse watch, expert Q&A.

### 📱 App — Builder side
- **Dashboard:** own health snapshot + watchlists (clients ↑, subbies ↓).
- **Post a job:** ongoing subcontract or day-hire; sees applicant licence/insurance/reliability before contact.
- **Verification centre:** badge application, criteria checklist, status, renewal.
- **Public profile manager:** photos, services, service area (claim-your-profile flow).
- **Reviews:** respond to reviews; request reviews from past clients (HiPages pattern).

### 📱 App — Homeowner side (Phase 2)
- Search directory → verified builders per trade/suburb; builder check (limited free); deposit-safety checklist; request quotes (lead engine).

### 🔧 Admin (you)
- Signal review queue (approve alerts before they fire — human-in-the-loop, accuracy discipline).
- Dispute/correction queue (48h SLA).
- Verification approvals; community moderation; metrics.

### Anti-patterns — confirmed by teardown, never do
Pay-per-dud-lead • expiring credits • 6-month lock-ins + stat-dec cancellation • review suppression • AI-only support • double-dipping fees.

## PART 3 — WHAT THIS CHANGES IN THE BUILD

Nothing in Phase 1 scope changes — but two additions earn their place in the sprint because they're cheap and high-leverage:
1. **Exposure Calculator** on the landing page (an hour of work, doubles lead capture).
2. **Public shareable result page** for every free builder check (turns every check into SEO + referral).

And one message change: our launch content should speak directly to **displaced Oneflare tradies** — "Oneflare closed. Before you just move platforms, get something no platform ever gave you: protection."
