How it works

From public record to your pocket in minutes

The warning signs of a collapse are public — court filings, administrator appointments, licence changes. They're just scattered across a dozen registers nobody has time to watch. We watch them all, fuse them per-ABN, and translate them into plain English.

  • Facts and opinion clearly separated — never rumours, never verdicts
  • Builders can dispute any record — corrections within 48 hours
